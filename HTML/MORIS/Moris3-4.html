HELLO, TAMARAWS!

FEURmoris is a student-driven magazine of FEU Roosevelt, celebrating the Tamaraw spirit through stories of creativity, unity, and growth.

This magazine isn't just about words and images, it's about us, our growth, and the spirit that unites us as Tamaraws.

TAKING THE NEXT CHAPTER

THE TRANSITIONS THAT DEFINE US

Every Tamaraw's story at FEU Roosevelt is a journey of transformation — where ambition meets opportunity and uncertainty to purpose. This feature follows seven Tamaraws, each with their own story of growth, resilience, and discovery.

Through friendships, shared challenges, and mentors who believed in them, they learned that transition isn't just about change — it's about becoming. Together, they embody the spirit of FEU that growth happens not alone, but side by side.

At FEU Roosevelt, every chapter ends with a new beginning and every Tamaraw carries forward the legacy of courage, connection, and transformation.

THE REBIRTH OF MARKETING

"Taking the Next Chapter the Tamaraw Way"

Before the pandemic, marketing thrived in the energy of face-to-face events, mall activations, and personal connections. Then 2020 arrived — and everything adapted. Digital screens became the new platforms, and algorithms replaced foot traffic.

From livestreams to hashtags, marketing found a new way to connect — one built on resilience, creativity, and community. Just like the Tamaraws, who turned their struggles into strength, their innovation into innovation.

This is more than a story about marketing's evolution — it's about how the Tamaraw spirit has always known what it means to adapt, inspire, and create in times of change.
